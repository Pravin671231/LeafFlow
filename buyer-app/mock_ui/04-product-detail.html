Monstera Deliciosa is native to the tropical rainforests of Southern Mexico and Panama. The plant is famous for its large, glossy leaves with natural holes and splits (called fenestrations). It grows as a climbing vine in the wild, but thrives beautifully as a houseplant.


This plant is a conversation starter — its bold silhouette adds drama to any space. It's also one of the easier tropicals to care for, tolerating occasional neglect and low light conditions better than most.
