🌿 About LeafFlow

Bringing the beauty of plants to every home since 2024

LeafFlow was born from a simple belief: every home deserves a little green. We're a Bengaluru-based plant nursery specialising in curated ornamental plants — carefully selected for health, beauty and ease of care.

Our botanists hand-pick each variety from trusted growers across Karnataka and Tamil Nadu. We take pride in shipping plants that arrive healthy, well-rooted and ready to thrive in your home.

Our Promise

  • 🌱 Healthy plants, guaranteed — if your plant arrives damaged, we'll replace it free
  • 📦 Eco-friendly packaging — biodegradable pots, recycled paper, zero plastic bubble wrap
  • 🌡️ Care card with every order — personalised care instructions for your exact plants
  • ☎️ Plant parent support — our team is here to help if your plant needs TLC

Our Team

We're a small team of plant enthusiasts, botanists and tech builders based in Bengaluru. Our nursery partners are located across Karnataka, Tamil Nadu and Andhra Pradesh — ensuring fresh, locally-grown plants delivered to your door.

🚚 Shipping Policy

Delivery Timeframes

  • Bengaluru: 1–2 business days
  • Other Karnataka cities: 2–3 business days
  • Rest of India: 3–5 business days

Shipping Charges

  • Free delivery on orders above ₹999
  • ₹79 flat shipping on orders below ₹999

Serviceable Areas

We currently deliver to Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Maharashtra, Delhi NCR, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Kerala, Gujarat and Rajasthan. We are expanding to more states — check back regularly!

Plant Care During Transit

All plants are packed with breathable eco-paper and biodegradable cushioning. We ship Monday–Friday to ensure plants aren't held over the weekend at courier facilities.

🔄 Returns & Refunds

7-Day Return Policy

We want you to be happy with your plants. If your order arrives damaged, unhealthy or incorrect, we'll make it right — no questions asked.

What qualifies for a return?

  • Plant arrived visibly damaged (broken stems, root damage)
  • Wrong plant delivered
  • Severe wilting or yellowing within 48 hours of delivery despite correct care

What is NOT eligible?

  • Natural leaf variation or minor cosmetic blemishes — these are normal for living plants
  • Damage caused by incorrect care after delivery
  • Change of mind after delivery

Refund Process

Approved refunds are processed within 3–5 business days back to your original payment method. To initiate a return, contact us at support@leafflow.com with your order number and photos.
